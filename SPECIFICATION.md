## 0. Verification Protocol, Mandatory Claim Classifications, and Finite-Progress Release Contract

In accordance with the TLQ Formalisation Protocol, every mathematical claim in this specification is assigned one of six strict classifications—separating deductive proof validity from empirical simulation regressions and assumption plausibility, and never using informal labels such as "verified," "discharged," or "green":

* **`PROVED`:** A complete deductive derivation establishes the stated conclusion from the explicitly stated definitions and axioms with no hidden hypotheses.
* **`PROVED UNDER ASSUMPTIONS`:** A complete deductive derivation establishes the conclusion, but relies on restrictive domain, moment, stationarity, or plant-invariance assumptions whose empirical applicability is a separate question.
* **`REFUTED`:** An explicit counterexample invalidates the claim as previously stated (retained in the audit register so regressions cannot recur).
* **`OPEN`:** The claim has not been established from the current axioms and requires additional mathematical machinery.
* **`CONJECTURE`:** A precisely stated, plausible mathematical claim awaiting complete proof.
* **`EMPIRICAL`:** Supported by simulation or numerical experiment rather than deductive proof.

## 2. Foundational Axioms & Separation of Causal vs. Oracle Optima

**Definition 2.1 (Filtered Stochastic Control Environment).**
A *stochastic control environment* is a tuple $\mathcal{E} = (\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \{\mathcal{F}_t\}_{t \ge 0}, \{\mathcal{G}_t\}_{t \ge 0}, \mathbb{P}, \mathcal{X}, \mathcal{U}, F)$ where:
1. $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P})$ is a probability space equipped with the **observable filtration** $\mathcal{F}_t = \sigma(h_t)$ ($h_t = (x_{0:t}, \xi_{1:t})$) and the **latent oracle filtration** $\mathcal{G}_t = \mathcal{F}_t \vee \sigma(w_{0:t})$ satisfying $\mathcal{F}_t \subseteq \mathcal{G}_t$.
2. $\mathcal{X} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ is the controlled state space containing the **survival domain** $\mathcal{X}_{\text{safe}} = \{x \in \mathcal{X} : B(x) > 0\}$ defined by a continuous barrier function $B : \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}$.
3. For every $x \in \mathcal{X}_{\text{safe}}$, $\mathcal{U}(x) \subset \mathbb{R}^m$ is a **non-empty, compact, convex** set of admissible actions containing $0 \in \mathcal{U}(x)$.
4. $F : \mathcal{X} \times \mathbb{R}^m \times \Xi \to \mathcal{X}$ is the measurable plant transition map $x_{t+1} = F(x_t, u_t, \xi_{t+1})$.

**Axiom 1 (Causal Admissibility vs. Oracle Measurability).**
1. The class of **causally admissible policies** $\Pi_{\text{adm}}$ consists of sequences $\pi = (\pi_0, \dots, \pi_{N-1})$ where each $u_t = \pi_t(h_t) \in \mathcal{U}(x_t)$ is strictly $\mathcal{F}_t$-measurable.
2. Conditional on $\mathcal{G}_t$, the shock $\xi_{t+1}$ is independent of $u_t$ and has finite third absolute moment $\mathbb{E}[\|\xi_{t+1}\|_2^3 \mid \mathcal{G}_t] < \infty$, oracle conditional mean $f_t^{\text{oracle}} := \mathbb{E}[\xi_{t+1} \mid \mathcal{G}_t]$, and oracle second-moment matrix $\Sigma_{2,t}^{\text{oracle}} := \mathbb{E}[\xi_{t+1}\xi_{t+1}^\top \mid \mathcal{G}_t] \succeq \sigma_{\min}^2 I_d \succ 0$.
3. The corresponding **causal observable moments** are the $\mathcal{F}_t$-measurable projections $\bar{f}_t := \mathbb{E}[\xi_{t+1} \mid \mathcal{F}_t] = \mathbb{E}[f_t^{\text{oracle}} \mid \mathcal{F}_t]$ and $\bar{\Sigma}_{2,t} := \mathbb{E}[\xi_{t+1}\xi_{t+1}^\top \mid \mathcal{F}_t] = \mathbb{E}[\Sigma_{2,t}^{\text{oracle}} \mid \mathcal{F}_t] \succeq \sigma_{\min}^2 I_d \succ 0$.

**Axiom 2 (Quadratic Stage Surrogate Structure).**
A stage objective $Q_t^{\text{quad}}(x_t, u; \theta_t)$ is *exactly quadratic with positive-definite curvature* if it has the form:
$$Q_t^{\text{quad}}(x_t, u; \theta_t) = c_0(x_t, \theta_t) + g_t(x_t, \theta_t)^\top u - u^\top \mathbf{\Phi}_t(x_t, \theta_t) u, \qquad \mathbf{\Phi}_t(x_t, \theta_t) \succeq \mu_{\min}(x_t) I_m \succ 0$$
where $\theta_t = (g_t, \mathbf{\Phi}_t)$ is either the oracle parameter $\theta_t^{\text{oracle}}$ ($\mathcal{G}_t$-measurable) or the causal parameter $\bar{\theta}_t$ ($\mathcal{F}_t$-measurable).

**Axiom 3 (Plant-Specific Passive Safe Invariance — Conditional Assumption).**
A control environment $\mathcal{E}$ satisfies *passive safe invariance* if the deterministic zero action $u_t = 0 \in \mathcal{U}(x_t)$ generated by the null hypergraph $G_{\text{null}}$ satisfies $B(F(x_t, 0, \xi_{t+1})) = B(x_t) > 0$ for all $x_t \in \mathcal{X}_{\text{safe}}$ and $\xi_{t+1} \in \Xi$. *(Note: This is a restrictive plant assumption that holds identically for frictionless inventory resizing where $W_{t+1} = W_t + 0 \cdot \xi_{t+1} = W_t$, but must be verified separately for any physical plant with drift.)*

**Lemma 2.2 (Quadratic Constrained Projection & Moreau Non-Expansiveness) — Status: `PROVED`**
* **Assumptions:** Definition 2.1(3) ($\mathcal{U}(x_t)$ non-empty, compact, convex) and Axiom 2 ($Q_t^{\text{quad}}$ quadratic with constant Hessian $-2\mathbf{\Phi}_t \prec 0$ in $u$).
* **Statement:** Let $u_{t,\text{unc}}^\star(\theta_t) := \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{\Phi}_t^{-1} g_t(x_t, \theta_t)$ be the unique unconstrained maximizer of $Q_t^{\text{quad}}(x_t, \cdot; \theta_t)$ on $\mathbb{R}^m$. Then:
  1. For all $u \in \mathbb{R}^m$, $Q_t^{\text{quad}}(x_t, u; \theta_t) = Q_t^{\text{quad}}(x_t, u_{t,\text{unc}}^\star; \theta_t) - \|u - u_{t,\text{unc}}^\star(\theta_t)\|_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t}^2$.
  2. The constrained maximization $\max_{u \in \mathcal{U}(x_t)} Q_t^{\text{quad}}(x_t, u; \theta_t)$ has a unique global maximizer $u_t^\star(\theta_t) \in \mathcal{U}(x_t)$ given by the $\mathbf{\Phi}_t$-orthogonal projection:
     $$u_t^\star(\theta_t) = \Pi_{\mathcal{U}(x_t)}^{\mathbf{\Phi}_t}\!\big(u_{t,\text{unc}}^\star(\theta_t)\big) := \arg\min_{u \in \mathcal{U}(x_t)} \big\| u - u_{t,\text{unc}}^\star(\theta_t) \big\|_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t}^2.$$
  3. By the first-order variational inequality for convex projections in the Hilbert space $(\mathbb{R}^m, \langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t})$, for every $u \in \mathcal{U}(x_t)$:
     $$Q_t^{\text{quad}}(x_t, u_t^\star(\theta_t); \theta_t) - Q_t^{\text{quad}}(x_t, u; \theta_t) \ge \big\| u - u_t^\star(\theta_t) \big\|_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t}^2,$$
     and for every unconstrained estimate $\hat{u}_t \in \mathbb{R}^m$, $\big\| \Pi_{\mathcal{U}(x_t)}^{\mathbf{\Phi}_t}(\hat{u}_t) - u_t^\star(\theta_t) \big\|_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t}^2 \le \big\| \hat{u}_t - u_{t,\text{unc}}^\star(\theta_t) \big\|_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t}^2$.
* **Scope Limitation:** Applies specifically to quadratic objectives $Q_t^{\text{quad}}$ (where the Hessian $-2\mathbf{\Phi}_t$ is independent of $u$). For non-quadratic strongly concave objectives, the exact equality in (1) is replaced by upper and lower Hessian sandwich bounds.

*Proof.* (1) Expand $(u - \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{\Phi}_t^{-1}g_t)^\top \mathbf{\Phi}_t (u - \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{\Phi}_t^{-1}g_t) = u^\top \mathbf{\Phi}_t u - g_t^\top u + \frac{1}{4}g_t^\top \mathbf{\Phi}_t^{-1} g_t = Q_t^{\text{quad}}(x_t, u_{t,\text{unc}}^\star) - Q_t^{\text{quad}}(x_t, u)$. (2) By (1), maximizing $Q_t^{\text{quad}}$ on $\mathcal{U}(x_t)$ is identical to minimizing $\|u - u_{t,\text{unc}}^\star\|_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t}^2$ on the non-empty compact convex set $\mathcal{U}(x_t)$. (3) For $u^\star = \Pi_{\mathcal{U}(x_t)}^{\mathbf{\Phi}_t}(u_{\text{unc}}^\star)$ and any $u \in \mathcal{U}(x_t)$, the projection variational inequality $\langle u_{\text{unc}}^\star - u^\star, \, u - u^\star \rangle_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t} \le 0$ implies $\|u - u_{\text{unc}}^\star\|_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t}^2 = \|u - u^\star\|_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t}^2 + \|u^\star - u_{\text{unc}}^\star\|_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t}^2 - 2\langle u_{\text{unc}}^\star - u^\star, u - u^\star \rangle_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t} \ge \|u - u^\star\|_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t}^2 + \|u^\star - u_{\text{unc}}^\star\|_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t}^2$, which by (1) rearranges to $Q_t^{\text{quad}}(x_t, u^\star) - Q_t^{\text{quad}}(x_t, u) \ge \|u - u^\star\|_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t}^2$. Non-expansiveness is immediate from $\langle \hat{u} - u_{\text{unc}}^\star, \Pi(\hat{u}) - \Pi(u_{\text{unc}}^\star) \rangle_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t} \ge \|\Pi(\hat{u}) - \Pi(u_{\text{unc}}^\star)\|_{\mathbf{\Phi}_t}^2$. $\square$

### The Financial Anchor Model: Exact vs. Surrogate Optimization

**Definition 2.3 (Bounded-Shock Frictionless Inventory Resizing Model $\mathfrak{P}_{\text{resize}}^{\text{bdd}}$).**
Fix horizon $N \in \mathbb{N}_+$, initial wealth $W_0 > 0$, safety margin $\eta_{\text{margin}} \in (0, 1)$, and **bounded shock support** $\Xi = [-\xi_{\max}, \xi_{\max}] \subset \mathbb{R}$ ($\xi_{\max} > 0$).
1. **Plant State & Admissible Exposure:** $x_t = (W_t, Q_t) \in \mathbb{R}_{>0} \times \mathbb{R}$, barrier $B(W_t, Q_t) = W_t > 0$, action $u_t = Q_{t+1} \in \mathcal{U}(W_t) := [-\Lambda_{\max} W_t, +\Lambda_{\max} W_t]$ where $\Lambda_{\max} := \frac{1 - \eta_{\text{margin}}}{\xi_{\max}}$, and dimensionless exposure $\tilde{\theta}_t := Q_{t+1}/W_t \in [-\Lambda_{\max}, \Lambda_{\max}]$.
2. **Dynamics & Pathwise Survival (`PROVED UNDER ASSUMPTIONS` — Bounded Support):** $W_{t+1} = W_t(1 + \tilde{\theta}_t \xi_{t+1})$. Because $|\xi_{t+1}| \le \xi_{\max}$ almost surely and $|\tilde{\theta}_t| \le \Lambda_{\max}$, $1 + \tilde{\theta}_t \xi_{t+1} \ge \eta_{\text{margin}} > 0$ almost surely, so $W_t \ge \eta_{\text{margin}}^t W_0 > 0$ on every sample path. *(Warning: If $\Xi = \mathbb{R}$ is unbounded sub-Gaussian rather than bounded by $\xi_{\max}$, pathwise survival does **not** hold for $\tilde{\theta}_t \neq 0$; only a probabilistic survival bound holds.)*
3. **Exact vs. Surrogate Objectives:**
   * *Exact Horizon-Normalized Log Objective ($\mathcal{P}_{\text{raw}}$):* $J_N^{\text{raw}}(\pi) := \frac{1}{N}\mathbb{E}\big[\sum_{t=0}^{N-1} \ln(1 + \tilde{\theta}_t \xi_{t+1})\big]$.
   * *Causal Quadratic Surrogate ($\mathcal{P}_{\text{cond}}^{\text{causal}}$):* $J_N^{\text{quad}}(\pi) := \frac{1}{N}\mathbb{E}\big[\sum_{t=0}^{N-1} (\bar{f}_t \tilde{\theta}_t - \frac{1}{2}\bar{\sigma}_t^2 \tilde{\theta}_t^2)\big]$, where $\bar{f}_t = \mathbb{E}[\xi_{t+1} \mid \mathcal{F}_t]$ and $\bar{\sigma}_t^2 = \mathbb{E}[\xi_{t+1}^2 \mid \mathcal{F}_t] \ge \sigma_{\min}^2 > 0$.

**Theorem 2.4 (Separation of Exact Log-Optimal Policy and Quadratic Surrogate Policy) — Status: `PROVED UNDER ASSUMPTIONS`**
* **Assumptions:** Model $\mathfrak{P}_{\text{resize}}^{\text{bdd}}$ (Definition 2.3) with exogenous shock process ($\gamma = 0, \eta_t = 0$) and $\bar{\sigma}_t^2 = \mathbb{E}[\xi_{t+1}^2 \mid \mathcal{F}_t] \ge \sigma_{\min}^2 > 0$.
* **Statement:**
  1. *(Exact Homothetic Bellman Separability):* For both $J_N^{\text{raw}}(\pi)$ and $J_N^{\text{quad}}(\pi)$ over $\pi \in \Pi_{\text{adm}}$, multi-period maximization decouples exactly into independent myopic maximizations over $\tilde{\theta}_t(h_t) \in [-\Lambda_{\max}, \Lambda_{\max}]$ at each epoch $t$.
  2. *(Distinct Optimizers, Constrained Variational Action-Distance Bound & Self-Contained Objective-Gap Bounds):*
     * The **exact causal log-optimal exposure** $\tilde{\theta}_{t,\text{log}}^{\text{causal}}(h_t) := \arg\max_{\tilde{\theta} \in [-\Lambda_{\max}, \Lambda_{\max}]} \mathbb{E}[\ln(1 + \tilde{\theta}\xi_{t+1}) \mid \mathcal{F}_t]$ is characterized by the variational inequality $\mathbb{E}\!\left[\frac{\xi_{t+1}}{1 + \tilde{\theta}_{t,\text{log}}^{\text{causal}}\xi_{t+1}} \,\middle|\, \mathcal{F}_t\right](\tilde{\theta} - \tilde{\theta}_{t,\text{log}}^{\text{causal}}) \le 0$ for all $\tilde{\theta} \in [-\Lambda_{\max}, \Lambda_{\max}]$ (reducing to $= 0$ when interior).
     * The **causal quadratic-surrogate optimal exposure** is $\tilde{\theta}_{t,\text{quad}}^{\text{causal}}(h_t) = \Pi_{[-\Lambda_{\max}, \Lambda_{\max}]}\!\big(\bar{f}_t / \bar{\sigma}_t^2\big)$, with inventory target $Q_{t+1,\text{quad}}^{\text{causal}} = W_t \tilde{\theta}_{t,\text{quad}}^{\text{causal}}$.
     * In general, $\tilde{\theta}_{t,\text{log}}^{\text{causal}}(h_t) \neq \tilde{\theta}_{t,\text{quad}}^{\text{causal}}(h_t)$. However, whether the maximizers lie in the interior or on the boundary of $\mathcal{I} := [-\Lambda_{\max}, \Lambda_{\max}]$, their action distance satisfies the constrained variational bound (with $\eta_{\text{margin}}$ to the **first** power in the denominator):
       $$\big|\tilde{\theta}_{t,\text{quad}}^{\text{causal}}(h_t) - \tilde{\theta}_{t,\text{log}}^{\text{causal}}(h_t)\big| \le \frac{\Lambda_{\max}^2 \, \mathbb{E}[|\xi_{t+1}|^3 \mid \mathcal{F}_t]}{\bar{\sigma}_t^2 \, \eta_{\text{margin}}} \le \frac{\Lambda_{\max}^2 \, \mathbb{E}[|\xi_{t+1}|^3 \mid \mathcal{F}_t]}{\sigma_{\min}^2 \, \eta_{\text{margin}}},$$
       and the exact-log-objective suboptimality satisfies both the **concave variational action-gap bound** and the **uniform third-order Taylor remainder bound**:
       $$J_N^{\text{raw}}(\pi_{\text{log}}^\star) - J_N^{\text{raw}}(\pi_{\text{quad}}^\star) \le \frac{1}{N}\sum_{t=0}^{N-1} \mathbb{E}\!\left[\min\!\left\{\frac{\Lambda_{\max}^4 \big(\mathbb{E}[|\xi_{t+1}|^3 \mid \mathcal{F}_t]\big)^2}{\bar{\sigma}_t^2 \, \eta_{\text{margin}}^2}, \; \frac{2 \Lambda_{\max}^3 \, \mathbb{E}[|\xi_{t+1}|^3 \mid \mathcal{F}_t]}{3 \eta_{\text{margin}}^3}\right\}\right] \le \frac{2 \Lambda_{\max}^3}{3 \eta_{\text{margin}}^3} \sup_{0 \le t < N} \mathbb{E}[|\xi_{t+1}|^3].$$

*Proof.*
1. *(Separability):* Follows from the additivity of $\sum_{t=0}^{N-1} r_t(\tilde{\theta}_t, \xi_{t+1})$ and the exogeneity of $\xi_{t+1}$ and $\mathcal{I} := [-\Lambda_{\max}, \Lambda_{\max}]$ with respect to $W_t, Q_t$.
2. *(Action-Distance Bound via Constrained Variational Inequalities):* Let $g_{\text{log}}(\tilde{\theta}) := \mathbb{E}[\ln(1 + \tilde{\theta}\xi_{t+1}) \mid \mathcal{F}_t]$ and $g_{\text{quad}}(\tilde{\theta}) := \bar{f}_t \tilde{\theta} - \frac{1}{2}\bar{\sigma}_t^2 \tilde{\theta}^2$, with unique maximizers $\theta_L := \tilde{\theta}_{t,\text{log}}^{\text{causal}} \in \mathcal{I}$ and $\theta_Q := \tilde{\theta}_{t,\text{quad}}^{\text{causal}} \in \mathcal{I}$. For any $\tilde{\theta} \in \mathcal{I}$, differentiating under the expectation (justified since $|\frac{\xi_{t+1}}{1 + \tilde{\theta}\xi_{t+1}}| \le \frac{\xi_{\max}}{\eta_{\text{margin}}}$ is integrable) and applying the algebraic identity $\frac{\xi}{1+\tilde{\theta}\xi} - (\xi - \tilde{\theta}\xi^2) = \frac{\tilde{\theta}^2 \xi^3}{1 + \tilde{\theta}\xi}$ together with $1 + \tilde{\theta}\xi_{t+1} \ge \eta_{\text{margin}} > 0$ a.s. yields:
   $$\big|g'_{\text{log}}(\tilde{\theta}) - g'_{\text{quad}}(\tilde{\theta})\big| = \left|\mathbb{E}\!\left[\frac{\tilde{\theta}^2 \xi_{t+1}^3}{1 + \tilde{\theta}\xi_{t+1}} \,\middle|\, \mathcal{F}_t\right]\right| \le \frac{\Lambda_{\max}^2 \, \mathbb{E}[|\xi_{t+1}|^3 \mid \mathcal{F}_t]}{\eta_{\text{margin}}}.$$
   Set $d := \theta_L - \theta_Q$. By the first-order optimality conditions for concave maximization on the convex interval $\mathcal{I}$:
   $$g'_{\text{log}}(\theta_L)(\theta_Q - \theta_L) = -g'_{\text{log}}(\theta_L) d \le 0 \iff g'_{\text{log}}(\theta_L) d \ge 0, \qquad \text{and} \qquad g'_{\text{quad}}(\theta_Q)(\theta_L - \theta_Q) = g'_{\text{quad}}(\theta_Q) d \le 0.$$
   Since $g'_{\text{quad}}$ is affine with slope $-\bar{\sigma}_t^2$, we have $g'_{\text{quad}}(\theta_L) - g'_{\text{quad}}(\theta_Q) = -\bar{\sigma}_t^2(\theta_L - \theta_Q) = -\bar{\sigma}_t^2 d$. Using $g'_{\text{quad}}(\theta_Q) d \le 0$ and $g'_{\text{log}}(\theta_L) d \ge 0$ in sequence gives:
   $$\bar{\sigma}_t^2 d^2 = \big(g'_{\text{quad}}(\theta_Q) - g'_{\text{quad}}(\theta_L)\big) d = \underbrace{g'_{\text{quad}}(\theta_Q) d}_{\le 0} - g'_{\text{quad}}(\theta_L) d \le -g'_{\text{quad}}(\theta_L) d \le \big(g'_{\text{log}}(\theta_L) - g'_{\text{quad}}(\theta_L)\big) d \le \big|g'_{\text{log}}(\theta_L) - g'_{\text{quad}}(\theta_L)\big| \cdot |d|.$$
   When $d = 0$, the action-distance bound holds trivially; when $d \neq 0$, dividing both sides by $\bar{\sigma}_t^2 |d| > 0$ yields $|d| = |\theta_L - \theta_Q| \le \frac{\Lambda_{\max}^2 \mathbb{E}[|\xi_{t+1}|^3 \mid \mathcal{F}_t]}{\bar{\sigma}_t^2 \eta_{\text{margin}}} \le \frac{\Lambda_{\max}^2 \mathbb{E}[|\xi_{t+1}|^3 \mid \mathcal{F}_t]}{\sigma_{\min}^2 \eta_{\text{margin}}}$.
3. *(Self-Contained Exact-Log Objective-Gap Bounds):* At each epoch $t$, the conditional stage suboptimality of $\theta_Q$ for the exact log objective is $\Delta g_t := g_{\text{log}}(\theta_L) - g_{\text{log}}(\theta_Q) \ge 0$. We establish both bounds independently:
   * *Concave Variational Bound (via Action Distance $d = \theta_L - \theta_Q$):* Because $g_{\text{log}}$ is concave on $\mathcal{I}$, $g_{\text{log}}(\theta_L) - g_{\text{log}}(\theta_Q) \le g'_{\text{log}}(\theta_Q)(\theta_L - \theta_Q) = g'_{\text{log}}(\theta_Q) d$. Subtracting the non-positive variational term $g'_{\text{quad}}(\theta_Q) d \le 0$ gives:
     $$\Delta g_t \le \big(g'_{\text{log}}(\theta_Q) - g'_{\text{quad}}(\theta_Q)\big) d \le \big|g'_{\text{log}}(\theta_Q) - g'_{\text{quad}}(\theta_Q)\big| \cdot |d| \le \frac{\Lambda_{\max}^2 \, \mathbb{E}[|\xi_{t+1}|^3 \mid \mathcal{F}_t]}{\eta_{\text{margin}}} |d| \le \frac{\Lambda_{\max}^4 \big(\mathbb{E}[|\xi_{t+1}|^3 \mid \mathcal{F}_t]\big)^2}{\bar{\sigma}_t^2 \, \eta_{\text{margin}}^2}.$$

## 8. Horizontal Dynamics ($\Rightarrow_{\mathcal{C}_t}$): Uniform All-Pairs Concentration & Scoped Theorems

**Definition 8.1 (Uniform All-Pairs Concentration Width $\kappa_{\text{search}}$ and Rewrite Law).**
Fix a finite candidate catalog $\mathbb{G}_0 \subset \mathbf{CD}(\Sigma)$ of cardinality $M_{\text{total}} = |\mathbb{G}_0| \ge 2$ (containing $G_{\text{null}}$), minimum dwell time $\tau_{\text{dwell}} \in \mathbb{N}_+$ ($\tau_{\text{dwell}} \ge 2$), window length $L \in \mathbb{N}_+$, and confidence schedule $\delta_k := \frac{6\delta}{\pi^2 k^2}$ at evaluation index $k = k_{\text{eval}} \in \mathbb{N}_+$.
1. **Empirical Window Score Difference:** For any pair $G_a, G_b \in \mathbb{G}_0$, let:
   $$\widehat{\Delta \mathcal{L}}_L(G_a, G_b; t) := \frac{1}{L}\sum_{\tau = t-L+1}^t \Big( \widehat{S}_{\tau-1}(G_a; \xi_\tau) - \widehat{S}_{\tau-1}(G_b; \xi_\tau) \Big), \qquad B_{\max} := 2\Lambda_{\max}\xi_{\max} + \frac{1}{2}\Lambda_{\max}^2 \xi_{\max}^2.$$
2. **All-Pairs Uniform Concentration Penalty (`PROVED` for Bounded Shocks):**
   $$\kappa_{\text{search}}(M_{\text{total}}, L, \delta_k) := 2 B_{\max} \sqrt{\frac{2 \ln(M_{\text{total}}^2 / \delta_k)}{L}} \qquad (\text{units: nats/step}).$$
3. **Certified Net Historical Gain:**
   $$\Delta \mathcal{G}_t(G_t \to G' \mid \mathcal{C}_t) := \widehat{\Delta \mathcal{L}}_L(G_t, G'; t) - \kappa_{\text{search}}(M_{\text{total}}, L, \delta_k) - \frac{\big|\Phi_{G'}(x_t) - \Phi_{G_t}(x_t)\big|}{\tau_{\text{dwell}}}.$$
4. **Deterministic Transition Rule $G_t \Rightarrow_{\mathcal{C}_t} G_{t+1}$:**
   * **Guard 1 (Collapse):** If $G_t \neq G_{\text{null}}$ and either $c(G_t \mid \mathcal{C}_t) = \mathtt{UNESTABLISHED}(\cdot)$ or $\Delta \mathcal{G}_t(G_t \to G_{\text{null}} \mid \mathcal{C}_t) > 0$, set $G_{t+1} = G_{\text{null}}$ and reset $\tau_{\text{elapsed}} \leftarrow 0$.
   * **Guard 2 (Expansion):** Else if $\tau_{\text{elapsed}} \ge \tau_{\text{dwell}}$ and $\exists G' \in \mathcal{M}(G_t) \subseteq \mathbb{G}_0$ with $c(G' \mid \mathcal{C}_t) \neq \mathtt{UNESTABLISHED}(\cdot)$ and $\Delta \mathcal{G}_t(G_t \to G' \mid \mathcal{C}_t) > \tau_{\text{hyst}} > 0$, set $G_{t+1} = \arg\max_{G'} \Delta \mathcal{G}_t(G_t \to G' \mid \mathcal{C}_t)$ and reset $\tau_{\text{elapsed}} \leftarrow 0$.
   * **Guard 3 (Persistence):** Else set $G_{t+1} = G_t$ and increment $\tau_{\text{elapsed}} \leftarrow \tau_{\text{elapsed}} + 1$.

### The Five Scoped Theorems and Propositions of Section 8

**Theorem 8.4a (Discrete-Time Switching Frequency Bound) — Status: `PROVED`**
* **Assumptions:** Definition 8.1(4) with integer dwell threshold $\tau_{\text{dwell}} \ge 2$ and initial timer $\tau_{\text{elapsed}}(0) = 0$.
* **Statement:** Over any discrete time interval $\{t_0, t_0 + 1, \dots, t_0 + T - 1\}$ of length $T \in \mathbb{N}_+$:
  1. Any two expansion transitions $t_{e,1} < t_{e,2}$ satisfy $t_{e,2} - t_{e,1} \ge \tau_{\text{dwell}} + 1$.
  2. Any collapse transition $t_c$ followed by an expansion transition $t_e > t_c$ satisfies $t_e - t_c \ge \tau_{\text{dwell}} + 1$.
  3. The total number of structural switches (expansions plus collapses) in $[t_0, t_0 + T - 1]$ is at most $2 \lfloor T / (\tau_{\text{dwell}} + 1) \rfloor + 2$.
* **Scope Limitation:** This theorem bounds **switching frequency only**; it does not by itself establish Lyapunov state stability or regret dominance.

*Proof.* When any switch (Guard 1 or Guard 2) fires at epoch $t_s$, the rule sets $\tau_{\text{elapsed}}(t_s + 1) = 0$. For Guard 2 to fire again at $t_e > t_s$, the persistence rule (Guard 3) must increment $\tau_{\text{elapsed}}$ at least $\tau_{\text{dwell}}$ times on epochs $t_s + 1, \dots, t_s + \tau_{\text{dwell}}$, so the earliest epoch at which $\tau_{\text{elapsed}}(t_e) \ge \tau_{\text{dwell}}$ is $t_e = t_s + \tau_{\text{dwell}} + 1$. Because Guard 1 sets $G_{t_c+1} = G_{\text{null}}$ and Guard 1 is disabled when $G_t = G_{\text{null}}$, two collapses cannot occur without an intervening expansion, proving (1)–(3). $\square$

**Theorem 8.4b (Conditional Emergency Fallback Safety) — Status: `PROVED UNDER ASSUMPTIONS` (Conditional on Axiom 3)**
* **Assumptions:** Axiom 3 (the environment satisfies passive safe invariance for $u = 0$) and Definition 8.1(4).
* **Statement:** If $x_t \in \mathcal{X}_{\text{safe}}$ and Guard 1 transitions to $G_{\text{null}}$, then $u_t(G_{\text{null}}) = 0 \in \mathcal{U}(x_t)$ has zero estimator variance ($\mathbf{V}_{G_{\text{null}}} = \mathbf{0}$) and preserves $B(x_{t+1}) = B(x_t) > 0$ pathwise. In $\mathfrak{P}_{\text{resize}}^{\text{bdd}}$, Axiom 3 holds unconditionally since $W_{t+1} = W_t + 0 \cdot \xi_{t+1} = W_t > 0$.

**Theorem 8.4c (Time-Uniform All-Pairs Concentration Under Adaptive Selection) — Status: `PROVED UNDER ASSUMPTIONS` (Bounded Shocks & Finite Catalog)**
* **Assumptions:** Model $\mathfrak{P}_{\text{resize}}^{\text{bdd}}$ (Definition 2.3) and Definition 8.1 with finite catalog $\mathbb{G}_0$ ($|\mathbb{G}_0| = M_{\text{total}} < \infty$).
* **Statement:** At each evaluation epoch $t_k$ ($k \in \mathbb{N}_+$), define the **all-pairs uniform failure event**:
  $$E_k^{\text{all}} := \left\{ \max_{(G_a, G_b) \in \mathbb{G}_0 \times \mathbb{G}_0} \left| \widehat{\Delta \mathcal{L}}_L(G_a, G_b; t_k) - \frac{1}{L}\sum_{\tau = t_k - L + 1}^{t_k} \big[\mathcal{L}_{\tau-1}(G_a \mid \mathcal{G}_{\tau-1}) - \mathcal{L}_{\tau-1}(G_b \mid \mathcal{G}_{\tau-1})\big] \right| > \kappa_{\text{search}}(M_{\text{total}}, L, \delta_k) \right\}.$$
  Then across an infinite horizon $N \to \infty$, regardless of overlapping windows and regardless of how the active pair $(G_{t_k}, G') \in \mathbb{G}_0 \times \mathbb{G}_0$ is adaptively selected:
  $$\mathbb{P}\!\left(\bigcup_{k=1}^\infty E_k^{\text{all}}\right) \le \sum_{k=1}^\infty \mathbb{P}\big(E_k^{\text{all}}\big) \le \sum_{k=1}^\infty \delta_k = \delta.$$

*Proof.* Fix evaluation index $k$ and any **deterministic** pair $(G_a, G_b) \in \mathbb{G}_0 \times \mathbb{G}_0$ ($G_a \neq G_b$). Because $G_a$ and $G_b$ are fixed elements of the catalog $\mathbb{G}_0$ (not data-selected random indices), for each $\tau \in \{t_k - L + 1, \dots, t_k\}$ their exposure estimates $\hat{\theta}_{\tau-1}(G_a), \hat{\theta}_{\tau-1}(G_b) \in [-\Lambda_{\max}, \Lambda_{\max}]$ are strictly $\mathcal{F}_{\tau-1}$-measurable. By Corollary 6.2b, the centered difference $\Delta m_\tau(G_a, G_b) := \big(\widehat{S}_{\tau-1}(G_a; \xi_\tau) - \widehat{S}_{\tau-1}(G_b; \xi_\tau)\big) - \big(\mathcal{L}_{\tau-1}(G_a \mid \mathcal{G}_{\tau-1}) - \mathcal{L}_{\tau-1}(G_b \mid \mathcal{G}_{\tau-1})\big)$ satisfies $\mathbb{E}[\Delta m_\tau(G_a, G_b) \mid \mathcal{G}_{\tau-1}] = 0$. Moreover, since $|\hat{\theta}_{\tau-1}| \le \Lambda_{\max}$ and $|\xi_\tau| \le \xi_{\max}$, $|\widehat{S}_{\tau-1}(G_a; \xi_\tau) - \widehat{S}_{\tau-1}(G_b; \xi_\tau)| \le 2\Lambda_{\max}\xi_{\max} + \frac{1}{2}\Lambda_{\max}^2 \xi_{\max}^2 = B_{\max}$, so $|\Delta m_\tau(G_a, G_b)| \le 2 B_{\max}$ almost surely. By the Azuma–Hoeffding inequality for bounded martingale difference sequences of length $L$:
$$\mathbb{P}\!\left( \left|\frac{1}{L}\sum_{\tau = t_k - L + 1}^{t_k} \Delta m_\tau(G_a, G_b)\right| > 2 B_{\max}\sqrt{\frac{2\ln(M_{\text{total}}^2 / \delta_k)}{L}} \right) \le 2 \exp\!\left(-\frac{2 L \kappa_{\text{search}}^2}{8 B_{\max}^2}\right) = \frac{2 \delta_k}{M_{\text{total}}^2}.$$
